# TASK 14 — Workforce & Attendance

Priority: P2
Status: ☑ Verified

## Required
1. Filters show active criteria.
2. Clear filters is always available when filters exist.
3. Attendance statuses are not distinguished by color alone.
4. Late/absent/present values have text labels.
5. Bulk actions require confirmation where destructive.
6. Pagination preserves filters.
7. Export actions indicate whether they use filtered or full data.
8. Mobile filter UI is accessible.
9. Date ranges are unambiguous in Arabic and English.
10. Permission-hidden actions do not leave broken spacing.
